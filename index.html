ABOUT US

More Than Just A Cyber Café.

DigiZone Cyber Café is a modern and customer-friendly cyber café located in MITC, Ayer Keroh, Melaka. We provide convenient access to computers, high-speed Internet and various digital services.

Our cyber café provides a comfortable environment for students, online gamers, freelancers, working individuals and members of the local community.
